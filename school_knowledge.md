## About the school

Northgate International School is a co-educational day school for students aged
4 to 18. We follow an inquiry-led curriculum with a strong focus on literacy,
numeracy, and wellbeing. Our school day is built around small classes, specialist
teachers, and a supportive parent community.

- **Main office:** +1 (555) 010-2200
- **Email:** office@northgate.example.edu
- **Address:** 18 Northgate Avenue, Riverton
- **Website:** https://northgate.example.edu

## School hours

- **Monday to Thursday:** 8:15 a.m. – 3:10 p.m.
- **Friday:** 8:15 a.m. – 2:00 p.m.
- **Early drop-off:** supervised from 7:30 a.m. in the main hall.
- **After-school care:** 3:15 p.m. – 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, in the library.
- **Main office hours:** 7:45 a.m. – 4:30 p.m., Monday to Friday.

The first bell rings at 8:10 a.m. Students should be in their classrooms by
8:15 a.m. Late arrivals must sign in at the main office.

## Attendance

- Report an absence before 9:00 a.m. by calling the attendance line at
  +1 (555) 010-2210 or emailing attendance@northgate.example.edu.
- Include the student's name, class, date, and reason for absence.
- Planned absences (family travel, medical appointments) should be requested at
  least five school days in advance through the main office.
- If a student is unwell, please keep them home until they have been fever-free
  for 24 hours without medication.
- Requests for homework during an absence can be sent to the class teacher via
  the main office.

## Daily life

- **Lunch:** a hot lunch service is available every day; menus are published
  monthly on the parent portal. Students may bring a packed lunch.
- **Uniform:** navy and grey with the school crest. Uniforms can be purchased
  from the school shop, open Tuesday and Thursday, 8:00 a.m. – 9:30 a.m.
- **Devices:** secondary students bring their own laptop or tablet. Primary
  students use school-provided devices.
- **Bus routes:** route maps and timetables are available from the transport
  office. Changes to a regular bus stop require two school days' notice.

## Health and safety

- Please inform the school nurse of any allergies, medical conditions, or
  medication requirements at the start of each school year.
- Students with a fever, vomiting, or a contagious illness should stay home.
- The school is a nut-aware environment. Please do not send nut products in
  packed lunches.
- Visitors must sign in at the main office and wear a visitor badge.

## Fees and payments

- Fee statements are issued at the start of each term.
- Payments can be made by bank transfer or through the parent portal.
- Families experiencing financial difficulty should contact the business manager
  confidentially through accounts@northgate.example.edu.
- Late payment enquiries are handled by the accounts team, not the class teacher.

## Communication

- The parent portal is the main source of school notices, reports, and calendar
  updates: https://portal.northgate.example.edu
- Teachers aim to reply to parent emails within two school days.
- For urgent matters during the school day, call the main office rather than
  emailing, so the message reaches the right person quickly.
- Weekly newsletters are emailed every Friday afternoon.

## Behaviour and wellbeing

- The school follows a restorative approach to behaviour and conflict.
- Bullying concerns should be raised with the class teacher or a deputy
  principal; all reports are taken seriously and handled confidentially.
- The wellbeing team offers short-term counselling and can refer families to
  external support services.
- Parents are welcome to request a meeting with the wellbeing team through the
  main office.

## Volunteering and community

- Parents are invited to join the Parent Association, which meets on the first
  Tuesday of each month at 6:30 p.m.
- Classroom volunteers must complete a short safeguarding briefing with the main
  office before helping on campus.
- The school welcomes guest speakers for career talks; contact the secondary
  office if you would like to take part.

## Common questions

**How do I report a late arrival?**
Sign in at the main office; the attendance team will update the register.

**Can I collect my child early?**
Yes. Come to the main office and sign them out. Please avoid collecting during
the final 20 minutes of the day unless it is urgent.

**How do I update my contact details?**
Email support@northgate.example.edu or update them in the parent portal.

**Where do I find lost property?**
The lost property box is in the main hall. Items are cleared at the end of each
term.

**How do I arrange a meeting with a teacher?**
Contact the class teacher by email or call the main office to book a time.

**Does the school provide before- and after-school care?**
Yes. Supervised early drop-off starts at 7:30 a.m., and after-school care runs
until 6:00 p.m. on school days.
