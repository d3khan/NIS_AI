## About the school

Nobles International School is a secondary school in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State,
Nigeria. The school describes itself as a Christian school where every child is
given a solid foundation for life's challenges in a pure Christian environment.

Its stated mission is to provide learning experiences that give children an edge
to positively affect their generation as future leaders, and to help them "make
heaven at last" (Revelation 5:10).

- The school has four academic departments.
- It adopts an international curriculum and collaborates with other reputable
  secondary schools around the world.
- It is registered with the British Council International School Award
  programme.
- Staff take part in regular in-house seminars and sponsored external courses.
- The school's core value is to train children in the way of the Lord, with the
  word of God taught daily.

## Academic programme

Nobles International School runs junior and senior secondary classes and enters
students for both national and international examinations.

### National examinations

- At the end of Junior Secondary Education, students sit the Basic Education
  Certificate Examination (BECE) for Akwa Ibom State and the National
  Examinations Council (NECO).
- At the end of Senior Secondary Education, students sit the Senior Secondary
  Certificate Examination (SSCE) conducted by WASSCE and NECO.

### International examinations

- At Junior Secondary level, students sit the Junior Cambridge Examination
  (Checkpoint), supervised by the British Council.
- At Senior Secondary level, the school prepares students for the International
  General Certificate of Secondary Education (IGCSE), the Scholastic Assessment
  Test (SAT), and the Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL).

### Assessment and promotion

- The school uses continuous assessment. Assessment scores are based on
  students' results, subject attendance, note updates, class work, practical
  projects, and assignments, and account for 30% of the overall assessment.
- The examination accounts for 70% of the overall assessment for each subject.
- School policy requires every student to pass Mathematics and English Language
  with an average benchmark of 70% before being promoted to the next class.
